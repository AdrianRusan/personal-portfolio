# Keyword research (OpenSEO, 2026-10-13)

**Verdict:** almost nobody searches for the exact offer ("claude code consultant", "ai code review service" have no measurable volume). Search earns its keep through the blog (Claude Code practitioner terms with real volume and low difficulty) and through three commercial terms we can put on the money pages. Bookings will still come mostly from LinkedIn and brand.

Source: OpenSEO project `adrian-rusan.com` (US market, `en`), `research_keywords` on 3 seeds + `get_keyword_metrics` on 30 candidates. Volumes are US monthly. KD = keyword difficulty, 0–100.
